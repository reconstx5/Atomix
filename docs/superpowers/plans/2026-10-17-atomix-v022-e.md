## Review Focus

1. **A provider that never answers on a restart** — the tuner keeps (or gives up on) the old address and the viewer isn't left with a dead stream forever (Task 1 test).
2. **Shutdown while a recording is starting** — no ffmpeg left running after `stop()` resolves (Task 1 test).
3. **Try again on a failed recording, then leaving the page** — the join finishes; the row moves to Done or Failed on the next read (Task 2 test).
4. **A Watch together member whose converted restart fails** — the room doesn't wait on it (Task 3 test).
5. **A connected server that is offline** — cached artwork still shows (Task 4 test).
