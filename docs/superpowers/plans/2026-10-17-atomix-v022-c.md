# Atomix Round 24 — Plan C (The spoken-word player and its pages; v0.22.0) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Audiobooks and podcast episodes play through the music bar in a "spoken" mode (skip back/forward, per-profile speed, sleep timer, chapters, one timeline per book), with a Podcasts library page, a podcast page and an audiobook page, offline play of downloaded books and episodes, and Plan C's v0.21 device-side leftovers.

**Architecture:** The music engine (`public/js/music.js`) keeps one queue and one `<audio>`; a queue entry may carry `spoken` metadata (book or episode, its parts, chapters and total length). A new DOM-free module `public/js/spoken-time.js` holds the arithmetic (book position ↔ part and offset, resume point, chapter at a time, "left" text, the sleep fade) so Node tests it; `public/js/spoken.js` builds entries and starts books and episodes. Two new views (`podcast.js`, `audiobook.js`) render those kinds; the library view gains the podcasts layout.

**Tech Stack:** plain ES-module browser JS; Node tests for the pure module; Playwright (1.56: `page.clock` for the sleep timer) in `devtools/v022-ui.mjs`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-atomix-v022-design.md` section 3, section 5 (Plan C leftovers).

## Global Constraints

- No dependencies. Older themes stay pixel-identical (`oldthemes-compare` "all pages match"): the bar looks the same for music; spoken mode appears only while a book or episode plays.
- Kids never see admin data: Subscribe, Check now, Keep, keep settings and feed status are admin-only (on a grown-up profile).
- Values (exact): skip back 15 s, forward 30 s (Settings → Profile can change them: back 5–60, forward 10–120); speeds 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2, 2.5, 3 (pitch kept); sleep 15/30/45/60 min or "End of chapter", a 10 s fade, "Add 10 min"; position saved every 10 s and on pause/leave; returning after more than 10 minutes rewinds 5 s; keys `J`/`L` (and Left/Right on the bar's seek slider) skip back/forward.
- Copy (exact): "Resume chapter N, X h Y min left" (minutes only under an hour: "Resume chapter 3, 12 min left"); "x h y min left" on the bar; "Play unplayed in order"; filters "Unplayed", "Downloaded", "All"; "Mark played" / "Mark unplayed"; "Mark finished"; "Sleep timer"; "Add 10 min"; "End of chapter"; "Subscribe"; "Check now"; "Downloading in another tab".
- Music keeps 1× and its own controls (shuffle, previous/next, repeat).

## Decisions this plan makes (spec silent or loose)

- **Speed and skips are profile prefs:** `spokenSpeed`, `spokenBack`, `spokenForward` added to `PREF_KEYS` (src/api/account.js), kept in range.
- **A multi-file book plays as its parts in the queue**, each entry carrying the book's `spoken` data; the bar shows book time (part start + time in part) and seeking past a part's end loads the right part. Progress goes to each part's id (the server maps it to the book, Plan B Task 4).
- **Server additions:** podcasts in `/api/libraries/:id/items` carry `newCount` (downloaded, unplayed for this profile); `GET /api/libraries/:id/new-episodes` (newest 20 such episodes across the library's shows); `POST /api/podcasts/check-all` (admin).
- **Sleep timer:** a state in `music.js` (`music.sleep`) driven by the page's timers; "End of chapter" ends at the next chapter start (or the part's end); the fade lowers `audio.volume` to 0 over the last 10 s, then pauses and restores the volume.
- **Leftover — old PIN offline:** the profiles list gains `pinStamp` (first 8 hex of a SHA-256 of the server's PIN hash, or null); a kept PIN check whose stamp differs is dropped, so the device asks to connect once.
- **Leftover — one tab downloads:** `navigator.locks` (`'atomix-downloads'`, `ifAvailable`); a tab without the lock shows "Downloading in another tab" on the Downloads page and doesn't run the queue.

## Review Focus

1. **A book whose parts have no duration yet (not probed) or a part file missing** — the bar shows the time it knows; a missing part is skipped with a toast, not a stalled player (Task 1 check).
2. **Changing speed, then playing a song** — the song plays at 1×; going back to the book restores its speed (Task 1 check).
3. **The sleep timer running when the queue ends or the person presses Stop** — the timer is cancelled; the volume is back where it was (Task 2 check).
4. **A Kids profile on a podcast page** — no Subscribe, Check now, Keep or feed status anywhere in the page's markup (Task 3 check).
5. **Two tabs open on a phone, one closed mid-download** — the other takes over the downloads within a few seconds (Task 5 check).
