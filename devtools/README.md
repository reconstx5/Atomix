# Browser checks (v0.28)

Small Playwright checks against a dev Atomix with made-up media. They need Node, ffmpeg, Playwright with Chromium
(`require('playwright')` must work) and, for picture comparisons, Python with Pillow.

1. `devtools/make-dev.sh <folder>` makes 8 test films and a 4-episode show, starts Atomix on :8787 and signs up
   `dallas` / `atomix-dev-1`.
2. Run a check: `node devtools/home-fn.mjs` (each prints PASS/FAIL lines and exits 1 on a failure).

| Check | What it covers |
|---|---|
| `home-fn.mjs` | Home: the spotlight follows the remote, the top area gets smaller past the first row, Enter and Back |
| `home-scroll.mjs`, `lib-scroll.mjs` | a wheel over the spotlight scrolls the rows / the grid; phones untouched |
| `lists-fn.mjs` | a playlist: Move down/up keeps the remote on the moved title; the playlist menu |
| `card-motion.mjs` | a rested card with no preview clip flips through its own pictures once; reduced motion; moving away stops it |
| `card-clip.mjs` | card motion v2, live preview: a film rested 1.2 s plays its 5 s silent clip in the art (one muted video, shown only after its first frame plays, gone with its src let go when focus moves, a key is pressed or the page hides); a held arrow asks for no clip; no clip, a 404 or a refused play() → the flip; a clip that never answers → given up after 2.5 s, the flip; a held arrow let go → the card landed on plays; Kids (a client stub: `state.profile.kids` is set in the page, not a real Kids profile; the server's Kids 404 is tested in `test/clips.test.js`) → the flip only, no video; pref off and saveData → nothing at all (no clip, no flip, no previews request); reduced motion → no video |
| `card-select.mjs` | card motion v2, selecting a card (Orbit + Arctic, desktop + phone): the art lifts (1.06, up 4 px), the glow shows, the row's other cards dim to 0.78, Orbit's info strip shows on the art's lower edge; the sheen once per card per visit; held arrow, reduce and none tiers; hover only with a mouse; rows never gain an up-and-down scroll |
| `card-open.mjs` | card motion v2, opening a title: the card's art travels into the title page's backdrop (or poster) and, where the card shows its title (a library grid), the title into the page title, names cleared after; the page's first blocks rise in 20 ms apart (≤ 200 ms, full motion only), the page staying above a growing backdrop; Back goes into the same card, focused, its row scrolled as before; reduce: crossfade only (no stagger); no view transitions (stubbed away): instant; a card off screen or gone (left out of Home's answer) on Back: a plain fade (vt-section), focus still restored (gone: the page's first control); a held key: no change; a backdrop held back 1 s (opened from a library grid): the art never travels into a backdrop that hasn't loaded. Artwork is stubbed (the dev media has none) |
| `card-load.mjs` | card motion v2, rows and loading (artwork stubbed and held back 800 ms): a tinted card paints its tint before the picture, its art box already full size; decode() then a 180 ms fade; Home's CLS 0; a picture already there when drawn shows at once (no fade, no tint); the first visit to Home and a library ripples the cards on screen (0, 20, 40 ms …, ≤ 300 ms, a fade and an 8 px rise), cards off screen don't move; no ripple on Back (library, Home) or when the page is drawn again; a card focused while the ripple runs shows at once; along a row a single press scrolls smoothly, a held key at once; reduce: the fade stays, the ripple is one fade without stagger or rise; none: nothing moves |
| `lib-fail.mjs` | a failed library filter request takes the spinner down and says so |
| `start-time.mjs` | how long until there is something to play: direct, converted, converted for Safari (HLS) |
| `iphone-channels.mjs` | v0.28: channels a device can't play — an iPhone (Widevine refused) doesn't list a Widevine-only channel on the wall, guide, On now or search; the switch, the message card (Watch on TV, Open TVNZ+, no stream asked for), zapping over it, a typed number; desktop Chrome unchanged; an admin's "Provider app link…" |
| `mock-iptv.mjs` | a tiny IPTV source on :9921 (TVNZ 2 Widevine only, Both Ways Widevine + ClearKey, Plain One) set as IPTV Simple's playlist through the admin API; used by `iphone-channels.mjs`, or `node devtools/mock-iptv.mjs serve [--plain]` while running `compare.mjs` (`--plain` leaves out the Widevine-only channel, which headless Chromium can't play and would hide). Ctrl+C puts IPTV Simple's settings back and removes the mock channels from the dev database |
| `fixed-compare.mjs` | v0.28: before/after for Live TV, the Guide and Home with recorded API answers and a fixed clock (the dev server's own channels change programme every ~40 s, so `compare.mjs` can't compare these pages): `record <before>` on the old code, `replay <after> <before>` on the new, then `diff <before> <after>` (markup and pixels, every theme, desktop and phone). Run it with `node devtools/mock-iptv.mjs serve --plain` going. `ART=1` (with `PAGES='#/,#/library/1,#/item/1'`) gives the titles on Home, a library grid and a title page stubbed artwork and tints (the title page a backdrop too) and captures once the pictures on screen and the backdrop have loaded: older themes at rest after load. Each page is loaded afresh and the app's service worker is blocked (it would answer past the stubs) |. `LIVE=1` (the Guide and Recordings on Preact) answers every `/api/live/` request from `live-fixtures.mjs` instead (nothing recorded, so `record` serves before and after; no mock IPTV needed), defaults `PAGES` to the guide, the guide on the 57th channel and Recordings, and takes `RECORDINGS=kids|error|empty`; it needs `BASE` (refuses unset or :8787) and evens out whitespace inside `style="…"` before writing the markup
| `guide-fn.mjs` | the Live TV guide's behaviour on the made-up Live TV (`live-fixtures.mjs`): one tab stop, `?channel=` (the cell marked `data-focus-now`), arrows in and off the window, a held arrow, Up/Down keeping the column, Earlier/Now/Later and the Day and Group pickers, more rows on scroll, the phone list and its last-pick-wins, the programme sheet, no channels, an unknown channel, no observers or intervals left after 20 visits. Legs by name; `SKIP=` leaves some out. Needs `BASE` (refuses unset or :8787) |
| `recordings-fn.mjs` | Recordings' behaviour on the made-up recordings: the five sections, Stop/Cancel/Delete/Remove/Try again/Stop series (the request and where focus lands), the in-row Delete question (Esc, one at a time), Try again polling until saved, rule options saving, Kids, a failed load or re-read, the 15-s re-read (not while asking or hidden), nothing left reading after the page is left. Legs by name; about 2 minutes (it waits out real 15-s re-reads) |
| `live-fixtures.mjs` | made-up Live TV for the Guide and Recordings checks: 60 channels (`fixtures/live/channels.json`: three groups, two favourites, an encrypted, an unplayable, a radio and a no-guide channel, catch-up on channel 2) with programmes built from the fixed clock `T_LIVE` (11 Nov 2026 19:10 NZ), and recordings in all five sections (`fixtures/live/recordings.json`). `routeLive(page, { recordings, channels, delay })` answers status, channels, groups, guide, programme and recordings (`kids` 403, `error` 500, `empty`); writes answer `{ ok: true }` and are listed by `liveRequests(page)` |
| `compare.mjs` | before/after for a screen moved to Preact: `capture <dir>` before the change and after, then `diff <a> <b>` (markup and pixels, every theme, desktop and phone). Needs `BASE` (refuses unset or :8787); the clock is fixed and the made-up title pages are included (below). `MUSIC=1` (pictures compared with a tolerance of 3 per colour channel: `TOLERANCE`, read by `imgdiff.py`) captures `MUSIC_PAGES` from `music-fixtures.mjs` instead (albums, artist, songs view, search, Now Playing in each state, the queue dialog, the book): every page starts from Home with the music stopped, plays the song paused at 2.5 s where the page needs it, moves inside the app, and the markup includes any open dialog, with whitespace inside `style="…"` evened out |
| `item-fixtures.mjs` | the made-up title pages (ids 900001–900042: a film with everything, part-watched, no art, a broken logo, a remote film, a show with three seasons, a show with no seasons, an empty season, an episode with an intro, a two-disc album, an artist, a book, a podcast, an audiobook) answered from `fixtures/items/<id>.json` with SVG pictures; `node devtools/fixtures/items/build.mjs` rewrites the JSON. `routeItems(page)` and `FIXTURE_PAGES` |
| `make-music.mjs` | `node devtools/make-music.mjs <mediaDir>` (needs ffmpeg) writes `<mediaDir>/Music/` (Cobalt Tide, *Low Water*, 2 discs of 3 + 2 songs; Fen Choir, *Small Hours*, 3 songs; 6-second tones with tags and a cover each; a synced `.lrc` for "Harbour Light", plain lyrics for "Night Ferry") and `<mediaDir>/Audiobooks/The Salt Road.m4b` (two 6-second chapters). Add a Music library on the first and an Audiobooks library on the second. Start the dev server with `ATOMIX_LRCLIB_BASE=http://127.0.0.1:9`, or songs with no lyrics ask lrclib.net |
| `music-fixtures.mjs` | the made-up music for the music checks: `guardBase()` (refuses unset `BASE` or :8787), `login(ctx)`, `ids(ctx)` (library, albums, artists, songs and the book, read from the API), `playAt(page, trackIds, index, seconds)` (plays, pauses and seeks to a fixed place; works with and without `music.started`), `playBookAt`, `openQueueDialog`, `routeMusicQuiet(page)` (no places written for the songs) and `MUSIC_PAGES(ids)`, the 13 pages `MUSIC=1 compare.mjs` captures (search is for "harbour light": plain "harbour" also finds the Live TV programmes, whose times move) |
| `item-users.mjs` | adds the non-admin user `guest` / `atomix-dev-guest` and, under it, a Kids profile "Kids" (dallas keeps one profile, so other checks still sign in without picking one). Running it again changes nothing |

The older browser suites and mock servers (mock IPTV, mock Chromecast) from earlier rounds were lost with a cloud
workspace; these replace part of them.
